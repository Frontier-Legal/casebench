An answer key is never finished. A statute changes, a key point lets a wrong answer through, a question turns out to be ambiguous. Correct it in Golden data. You need the editor role.

<Steps>

1. Choose **Golden data**, find the case, and click its row. Search narrows a long list.

2. Change what needs changing. Here a lawyer adds a reference answer, the precedent, and a key point that costs an answer giving the wrong limitation period.

3. Write **why** you change it. The reason is required: the change log keeps it with your name and the time.

4. Choose **Save the change**.

</Steps>

## When it takes effect

- **By default**, a change takes effect when you save it. The suite gets a new fingerprint, so every later report says it tested the corrected case.
- **When your team requires a second editor**, your change waits in other editors' queues. They see each field before and after, your name, the time and your reason, and approve it or reject it with a note. Nobody approves their own change.

## The change log

Every change, approval and rejection is kept: who, when, before, after and why. On a team server, editors, engineers and admins read it under **Audit log**. Nothing in it can be changed or removed.
