Your team's workbench runs on a server your firm controls. There is no password to remember: you sign in with a link sent to your email, or with your Google or Microsoft account.

## The first time

Your admin invites you with your work email and a role. The email names how many items wait for you and about how long they take, and holds a sign-in link. Click it and the workbench opens on your queue.

A sign-in link works **once**, and only **within 15 minutes**. That is deliberate: a forwarded or old link cannot open your account.

## Later

Open the workbench's address. If you are signed out:

- enter your email and choose **Email me a sign-in link**, then click the link in the email; or
- choose Google or Microsoft, when your firm has set them up, with the same email the admin invited.

A sign-in lasts 14 days on that browser.

## When the server sends no email

Some firms run the server without email. Then the sign-in page says so, the button reads **Ask for a sign-in link**, and your admin gives you a link from the Team page instead. It follows the same rules: once, within 15 minutes.

## On a colleague's machine

When an engineer opens the workbench on their own computer instead of a team server, there is no sign-in. You type your name, which is recorded with every verdict, and choose **I review legal answers**.
